# Lesson Recording Policy

**Effective date:** 24 July 2026

EduVibe Hub lessons are not recorded by default.

A lesson may be recorded only when:

- The parent receives a clear explanation of the purpose.
- The parent gives separate affirmative consent.
- The educator agrees.
- The recording is limited to the agreed purpose and period.

Learners, parents and third parties may not independently record, photograph, livestream or publish a lesson without permission.

An approved recording must:

- Be accessed only by authorised people.
- Be stored securely.
- Not be posted publicly without separate media consent.
- Be deleted when the agreed purpose or retention period ends.

A parent may withdraw consent for future recording. Withdrawal does not invalidate lawful use that occurred before withdrawal.
