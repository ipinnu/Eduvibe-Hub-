# Cookie Policy

**Effective date:** 24 July 2026

EduVibe Hub does not currently use advertising cookies or behavioural tracking cookies on its public website.

Essential technical storage may be used where required for security, form operation or basic website functionality. Essential technology cannot be disabled through a consent preference when it is strictly necessary to provide the requested service.

If analytics, advertising pixels, embedded third-party tools or other non-essential tracking are introduced, this policy will be updated and any required consent choice will be provided before those technologies are activated.
