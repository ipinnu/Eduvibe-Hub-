# Child Safeguarding Policy

**Effective date:** 24 July 2026

EduVibe Hub is committed to protecting every learner from abuse, exploitation, harassment and inappropriate conduct during online tutoring.

## Core rules

- Enrolment and administrative communication must involve a parent, guardian or authorised adult.
- Educators must maintain professional boundaries.
- Private social-media contact with learners is prohibited.
- Sexual, discriminatory, threatening, humiliating or abusive conduct is prohibited.
- Lessons must take place in appropriate, observable environments.
- Personal information about a learner must be limited to what is necessary.

## Parent presence

- For learners aged 3–6, a responsible adult should remain nearby and available throughout the lesson.
- For learners aged 7–12, a responsible adult must be reachable and should periodically monitor the learning environment.
- A parent may remain present during any lesson.

## Communication

Lesson scheduling, payment, complaints and personal matters must be discussed with the parent. Any necessary learner communication should occur through an approved lesson platform or a conversation that includes the parent.

## Recording and media

Lessons are not recorded by default. No participant may record, photograph or publish a lesson without prior permission and the required consent.

## Safeguarding concerns

If an educator observes or receives information suggesting that a child may be at risk:

1. The educator will listen calmly without promising secrecy.
2. Only necessary questions will be asked.
3. A factual written record will be made.
4. The concern will be reported through the appropriate safeguarding channel.
5. Emergency services or relevant authorities may be contacted where a child faces immediate danger or reporting is legally required.

## Ending a lesson

An educator may pause or end a lesson where:

- The environment appears unsafe.
- An unauthorised person disrupts the session.
- A participant behaves inappropriately.
- Continuing could place the learner or educator at risk.

The parent will be contacted as soon as reasonably possible.

## Reporting

Safeguarding concerns may be reported through the contact details on the EduVibe Hub website or by WhatsApp at **+234 907 314 9749**. Immediate danger should be reported directly to the appropriate emergency or child-protection authority.

## Confidentiality

Safeguarding information is shared only with people who need it to protect the child, investigate the concern or meet a legal duty.
