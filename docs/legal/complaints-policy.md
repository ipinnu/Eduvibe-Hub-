# Complaints Policy

**Effective date:** 24 July 2026

EduVibe Hub aims to resolve complaints fairly, respectfully and without unnecessary delay.

## How to complain

A parent may complain through the website contact details or by WhatsApp at **+234 907 314 9749**.

Please include:

- Parent's name.
- Learner's first name where relevant.
- What happened and when.
- The preferred outcome.
- Any supporting information.

## Response times

- Receipt will normally be acknowledged within two business days.
- EduVibe Hub will aim to provide a substantive response within ten business days.
- If more time is required, the parent will receive an explanation and revised response date.

## Review

The complaint will be reviewed by someone able to consider the lesson records, communications and applicable policies. Everyone involved will be treated fairly and personal information will be limited to those who need it.

## Safeguarding and privacy complaints

Safeguarding concerns are handled urgently under the Child Safeguarding Policy. Privacy complaints are handled under the Privacy Policy and may be escalated to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

## Unresolved complaints

If a consumer complaint cannot be resolved directly, the parent may seek independent advice or contact the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission.

Making a genuine complaint will not result in unfair treatment of a learner.
