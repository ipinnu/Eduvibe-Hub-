# Online Lesson Code of Conduct

**Effective date:** 24 July 2026

This code applies to educators, parents, guardians and learners participating in EduVibe Hub lessons.

## Everyone must

- Communicate respectfully.
- Avoid discriminatory, abusive, sexual or threatening language.
- Protect lesson links, passwords and personal information.
- Avoid unauthorised photographs, screenshots or recordings.
- Use lesson materials only for the learner's personal education.
- Report safety or privacy concerns promptly.

## Educators must

- Maintain professional boundaries.
- Teach from an appropriate environment.
- Use age-appropriate language and materials.
- Communicate administrative matters through the parent.
- Avoid private social-media relationships with learners.
- Report safeguarding concerns.

## Parents and guardians must

- Provide accurate enrolment information.
- Ensure the learner has a safe and reasonably quiet lesson space.
- Remain available according to the learner's age and needs.
- Inform the educator of absences or relevant support needs.
- Avoid completing work or answering questions for the learner during lessons.

## Learners should

- Join on time with required materials.
- Treat the educator and others respectfully.
- Participate without sharing inappropriate content.
- Ask for help when confused or uncomfortable.

## Breaches

EduVibe Hub may issue a warning, pause a lesson, involve the parent or end tutoring where this code is breached. Serious safety concerns may be reported to the appropriate authority.
