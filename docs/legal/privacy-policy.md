# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 24 July 2026

EduVibe Hub provides online tutoring for children. This policy explains how we handle information about parents, guardians and learners.

## Information we collect

We may collect:

- A parent or guardian's name, telephone number and contact details.
- A learner's first name, age, year group and learning needs.
- Subject interests, preferred lesson days and information voluntarily provided by a parent.
- Enrolment, attendance, payment, lesson and progress-report records.
- Messages sent through the website or WhatsApp.

Parents should not provide medical, identification or other sensitive information unless it is necessary for the learner's safety or support.

## How we use information

We use information to:

- Respond to enquiries and arrange calls or lessons.
- Assess learning needs and personalise tutoring.
- Manage schedules, payments, attendance and parent updates.
- Protect learners and respond to safeguarding concerns.
- Meet legal, accounting and consumer-protection obligations.
- Improve the service using non-identifying feedback.

We do not sell personal information.

## Children's information

EduVibe Hub only accepts enrolment and consent from a parent, legal guardian or authorised adult. Children should not submit personal information independently.

We collect only information reasonably required to provide tutoring and protect the learner. Marketing, photography, testimonials and lesson recording require separate permission.

## Legal grounds

Information may be processed because:

- It is necessary to respond to a request or provide tutoring.
- A parent or guardian has given consent.
- EduVibe Hub must meet a legal obligation.
- It is necessary to protect a learner or another person's vital interests.
- EduVibe Hub has a legitimate operational interest that does not override the family's rights.

Consent may be withdrawn at any time, although this does not invalidate earlier lawful use.

## Sharing information

Information may be shared only when necessary with:

- The assigned educator.
- Service providers supporting communications, payments or website operation.
- Professional advisers.
- Regulators, courts or safeguarding authorities where legally required.

We require anyone handling information for EduVibe Hub to protect it and use it only for the agreed purpose.

## Retention

- Unsuccessful or inactive enquiries are normally deleted after 12 months.
- Active family and lesson records are kept while tutoring continues.
- Payment and contractual records may be retained for up to six years where required for legal or accounting purposes.
- Safeguarding records may be retained longer when necessary to protect a child or meet legal duties.
- Optional media is removed from future use after valid consent is withdrawn, where reasonably possible.

## Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Parents should avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information through ordinary messaging services.

## Your rights

Subject to applicable law, a parent, guardian or data subject may request:

- Access to personal information.
- Correction of inaccurate information.
- Deletion where retention is no longer necessary.
- Restriction or objection to certain uses.
- Withdrawal of consent.
- A portable copy where applicable.
- Information about a suspected personal-data breach.

## Complaints and contact

Privacy questions and requests may be submitted through the contact details published on the EduVibe Hub website or by WhatsApp at **+234 907 314 9749**.

Unresolved data-protection complaints may be referred to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

## Policy changes

Material changes will be published with a revised effective date. Where a change materially affects enrolled families, reasonable notice will be provided.
