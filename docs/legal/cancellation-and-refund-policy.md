# Cancellation and Refund Policy

**Effective date:** 24 July 2026

This policy states the rules for missed lessons, rescheduling, package expiry, cancellations and refunds.

## Parent cancellations

- A lesson may be rescheduled without charge when at least 12 hours' notice is provided.
- Requests made with less than 12 hours' notice may be treated as a used lesson.
- Genuine emergencies will be considered fairly on a case-by-case basis.
- A learner who does not join within 15 minutes and whose parent has not contacted the educator may be marked absent.

## Rescheduling

- A rescheduled lesson is subject to educator availability.
- A lesson may normally be rescheduled once.
- Repeated rescheduling may require the family to select a new regular time.

## EduVibe Hub cancellations

When EduVibe Hub cancels a paid lesson, the family may choose:

- A replacement lesson at a mutually suitable time; or
- A refund or account credit for the affected lesson.

The family will not lose a paid lesson because the educator was unavailable.

## Technical problems

- If EduVibe Hub's technical failure prevents most of a lesson from taking place, the lesson will be repeated or credited.
- If the family's device or connection prevents the lesson, the educator will make reasonable efforts to continue, but the lesson may still be treated as used.
- Where responsibility is unclear, the parties should agree on a fair replacement or partial credit.

## Packages and expiry

- Lesson packages are valid for 90 days from the first scheduled paid lesson unless another period is stated before purchase.
- EduVibe Hub may extend a package for illness, emergency or another reasonable circumstance.
- Families should request an extension before the package expires where possible.

## Refunds

- A parent may request cancellation of unused lessons.
- Lessons already delivered or missed without sufficient notice are not refundable.
- Any package discount may be recalculated based on the number of lessons already used before the refund balance is determined.
- Approved refunds will normally be processed within 10 business days using the original payment method where reasonably possible.
- No policy term removes a consumer right that applies under Nigerian law.

## How to request a cancellation or refund

Requests should be sent through the contact details on the EduVibe Hub website or by WhatsApp at **+234 907 314 9749**, stating the parent's name, learner's first name, affected lesson and requested resolution.
